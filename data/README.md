# Data

Nine files are read by the analysis. Four sit in `data/` and are the public downloads with
Windows line endings converted to Unix; no rows are dropped, no columns selected and no
values rescaled. Five sit in `data/intl/` and are written by `src/prepare_data.py` from the
archives in `raw/`.

## Rebuilding and verifying

```bash
cd src
python prepare_data.py          # rebuild from raw/, verify, write MANIFEST.txt
python prepare_data.py --check  # verify the shipped files against raw/, write nothing
```

`MANIFEST.txt` is generated by that script and lists rows, span, size and SHA-256 for every
file. The derived files are written with `float_format="%.17g"` and read back with
`float_precision="round_trip"`; `prepare_data.py` exits non-zero if any file fails to
round-trip exactly.

Vintages below are given as the last observation present in the committed file. All nine
sources were downloaded in mid-2026.

## `data/`

### `gsw_yields_daily.csv` — US Treasury zero-coupon yields
- **Source.** Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, "The U.S. Treasury Yield
  Curve: 1961 to the Present", FEDS working paper 2006-28.
- **URL.** <https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/nominal-yield-curve.htm>
- **Citation.** Gürkaynak, R.S., Sack, B. and Wright, J.H. (2007), *Journal of Monetary
  Economics* 54(8), 2291–2304.
- **Vintage.** Daily, 1961-06-14 to 2026-05-29 (16,948 rows).
- **Transform.** `raw/feds200628.csv` carries a prose preamble and roughly a hundred
  columns. The header row is located, and the date plus `SVENY01`–`SVENY10` are kept.
- **Read by.** `cp_core.load_yields`, which uses `SVENY01`–`SVENY05`.
- **Licence.** Work of the US federal government; free to use with attribution.

### `F-F_Research_Data_5_Factors_2x3_daily.csv` — Fama–French five factors
- **Source.** Kenneth R. French Data Library, Tuck School of Business, Dartmouth.
- **URL.** <https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html>
- **Citation.** Fama, E.F. and French, K.R. (2015), *Journal of Financial Economics*
  116(1), 1–22.
- **Vintage.** Daily, 1963-07-01 to 2026-04-30. Percent returns.
- **Read by.** `cp_core.load_ff5` for HML and RF; `cp_core.load_ff5_all` for all five
  factors in `factor_alpha.py`.

### `Portfolios_Formed_on_BE-ME.csv` — portfolios sorted on book-to-market
- **Source, URL and licence.** As above.
- **Vintage.** Monthly returns 1926-07 to 2026-04; the annual value-weighted average BE/ME
  characteristic 1927 to 2025.
- **Read by.** `spread.load_value_spread`, which forms `log(BE/ME[Hi 10] / BE/ME[Lo 10])`,
  dates it at June of each year and forward-fills.

### `6_Portfolios_2x3_Daily.csv` — six size × book-to-market portfolios
- **Source, URL and licence.** As above.
- **Vintage.** Daily, 1926-07-01 to 2026-04-30.
- **Read by.** `cp_core.load_ff_legs`, which reconstructs the two HML legs as
  `value = ½(SMALL HiBM + BIG HiBM)` and `growth = ½(SMALL LoBM + BIG LoBM)`, used by
  `duration_test.py` and `data_summary.py`.

## `data/intl/`

### `yields_UK.csv` — Bank of England nominal gilt curve
- **Source.** Bank of England, government liability curve (nominal), daily spot rates.
- **URL.** <https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/statistics/yield-curves>
- **Raw.** `raw/glcnominalddata.zip`, eight workbooks spanning 1979 to present.
- **Vintage.** Daily, 1979-01-02 to 2026-05-29 (11,966 rows).
- **Transform.** In each workbook the nominal spot sheet is located, the maturity row
  labelled `years:` is found, and the column nearest each of 1–5 years is taken; quoted
  maturities are not exactly integer years. Workbooks are concatenated and duplicate dates
  resolved to the later file.
- **Licence.** Open Government Licence v3.0.

### `yields_CA.csv` — Bank of Canada zero-coupon curve
- **Source.** Bank of Canada, zero-coupon bond yields.
- **URL.** <https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/interest-rates/bond-yield-curves/>
- **Raw.** `raw/yield_curves.csv`, all maturities from 3 months to 30 years.
- **Vintage.** Daily, 1986-01-02 to 2026-06-03 (9,923 rows).
- **Transform.** Columns `ZC100YR`, `ZC200YR`, `ZC300YR`, `ZC400YR` and `ZC500YR` kept and
  multiplied by 100; the source is in decimals and the rest of the project in percent.
- **Licence.** Bank of Canada terms of use; reproduction permitted with attribution.

### `yields_JP.csv` — Japanese government bond yields
- **Source.** Japan Ministry of Finance, JGB interest rates. These are constant-maturity
  coupon yields, not zero-coupon yields as in the other three markets.
- **URL.** <https://www.mof.go.jp/english/policy/jgbs/reference/interest_rate/>
- **Raw.** `raw/jgbcme_all.csv`.
- **Vintage.** Daily, 1974-09-24 to 2026-05-29 (12,578 rows).
- **Transform.** The `1Y`–`5Y` columns kept.

### `yields_DE.csv` — Bundesbank Svensson curve
- **Source.** Deutsche Bundesbank, term structure of interest rates, Svensson estimates.
- **URL.** <https://www.bundesbank.de/en/statistics/time-series-databases>
- **Raw.** `raw/bbk_paket1.csv`.
- **Vintage.** Monthly at source, 1972-09-30 to 2026-05-31 (645 rows).
- **Transform.** Rows matching `YYYY-MM` kept and dated to month end; the 1–5 year spot
  columns extracted by position.

### `hml_daily_intl.csv` — international value factors
- **Source.** AQR Capital Management, "The Devil in HML's Details" daily factor set, sheet
  `HML FF`, which is the standard Fama–French construction.
- **URL.** <https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Datasets>
- **Citation.** Asness, C.S. and Frazzini, A. (2013), *Journal of Portfolio Management*
  39(4), 49–68.
- **Raw.** `raw/The Devil in HMLs Details Factors Daily.xlsx`.
- **Vintage.** Daily to 2026-03-31. Markets start on different dates and NaNs are
  preserved so each series drops on its own: Canada 1984-07-02, UK and Japan 1988-07-01,
  Germany 1990-07-02.
- **Transform.** Columns `GBR`, `CAN`, `JPN` and `DEU` kept and multiplied by 100; the
  source is in decimals.
- **Licence.** Free for academic and research use with citation; see AQR's data terms.
