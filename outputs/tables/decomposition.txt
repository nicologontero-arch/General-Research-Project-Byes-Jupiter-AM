================================================================================================
Timing vs tilt decomposition, with t-stats
E[active] = Cov(w,h) + (E[w]-1)E[h] - costs; the exact split behind both alpha definitions
================================================================================================
timing = Cov(w,h). tilt = (E[w]-1)E[h]. total = the benchmark-difference alpha.
|resid| is total minus the three terms, and is zero to machine precision.
