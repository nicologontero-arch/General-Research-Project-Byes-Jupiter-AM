================================================================================================
Factor-model alpha - strategy return regressed on HML
r_strategy = alpha + beta * r_HML + e (Newey-West), against the benchmark-difference alpha reported everywhere else
================================================================================================
alpha_reg is the regression intercept, beta estimated. alpha_diff is mean(timed) -
mean(HML), beta forced to 1. Both annualised percent. The last column is their gap,
which equals (beta - 1) * E[r_HML].
