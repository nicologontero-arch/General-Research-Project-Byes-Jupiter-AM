================================================================================================
S agreement: the same four checks
truncation, rolling stability, timing versus tilt, and spanning against both the yield curve and its own two ingredients
================================================================================================
w = 1 + c (1 + 0.75 d sign(c)),  c = tanh(z_CP),  d = 2(p - 0.5)
The multiplier 1 + 0.75 d sign(c) lies in [0.25, 1.75] and scales conviction only.
Baseline: n=204, 1975-09-30 to 2026-06-30, alpha +2.34%/yr, IR 0.37, NW t 2.47
